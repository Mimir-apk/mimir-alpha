## Native runtime

`render_fps_helper` is linked with NDK 27.0.12077973's static C++ runtime. Its libc++, libc++abi,
libunwind and compiler-rt licensing is recorded in [libc++](dependencies/LICENSE-libcxx.txt),
[libc++abi](dependencies/LICENSE-libcxxabi.txt), [libunwind](dependencies/LICENSE-libunwind.txt) and
[compiler-rt](dependencies/LICENSE-compiler-rt.txt). These texts include LLVM exceptions and legacy
runtime terms. The NDK toolchain's LLVM base is `3c92011b600bdf70424e2547594dd461fe411a41`.
The full NDK distribution notice is also retained in `NOTICE-NDK-toolchain.txt`.

Android's libc, libdl, liblog and libm are loaded from the device operating system; they are not
copied into the helper executable. The native hashing reader's vendored C components are listed in
the NOTICE in this folder and their source-tree license files.
