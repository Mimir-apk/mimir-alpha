# Audio sources

The frontend music is generated by Mimir's scripts:

| File in `src/main/res/raw/` | Generator |
|---|---|
| `mimir_the_well_remembers_v22.ogg` | `tools/render_the_well_remembers.py` |
| `mimir_frontend_ambient_v1.ogg` | `tools/render_frontend_ambient.py` |

The six UI sounds are adaptations of **Interface Sounds 1.0**, created/distributed by
[Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Shipped sound | Original | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| `ui_nav_tick.ogg` | `tick_001.ogg` | Leading-silence trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |
| `ui_back.ogg` | `back_001.ogg` | Trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |
| `ui_confirm.ogg` | `confirmation_003.ogg` | Trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |
| `ui_context.ogg` | `switch_003.ogg` | Trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |
| `ui_error.ogg` | `error_008.ogg` | Trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |
| `ui_toggle.ogg` | `toggle_004.ogg` | Trim, low-pass filter, fades and lower volume |

CC0 permits modification and commercial use. Kenney credit is included as attribution of the source.
